📖 HOW TO PLAY

🏀 Natural Bounce & Dribble: The basketball bounces rhythmically as you roll. Time your hops to build speed and rhythm!
🚀 Super Leap / High Jump: Press [SPACE], [W], or [UP] when landing to boost your bounce sky-high over tall spike hurdles!
💥 Ground Slam: Press [S] or [DOWN] while in midair to slam down at top speed! Rebounds with a shockwave that crushes nearby defenders!
⚡ Trampolines & Springs: Hit the yellow spring pads to catapult yourself across massive chasms and spiked ceilings!
👾 Stomp Defenders: Land on top of patrolling walkers or bat defenders from above to stomp them for +200 PTS!
🎯 THE FINAL SHOT: Reach the Buzzer Beater Zone at the court's end. Drag & release or hold [SPACE] to aim and sink the game-winning shot into the hoop!
