# 🏀 Basketball Bounceback — Game Jam Edition

A fast-paced arcade basketball platformer where you dribble, bounce, super-jump over obstacles, stomp defenders, and sink the clutch buzzer-beater shot into the championship hoop!

![Basketball Bounceback](basketball_preview.png)

## 🎮 Play Instantly
- **Windows**: Double-click `Play Game.bat`
- **Any Platform / Web**: Open `index.html` in any modern web browser (Edge, Chrome, Firefox, Safari).
- **Zero Install / Offline**: All audio and graphics are synthesized and embedded — no server or downloads required.

---

## 🕹️ Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Touch / Mobile |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Move Left / Right** | A / D or ← / → | On-screen ◀ / ▶ pads |
| **Super Jump (High Bounce)** | Space / W / ↑ | On-screen ▲ (Jump) pad |
| **Ground Slam (Pound)** | S or ↓ (while in mid-air) | On-screen ▼ (Slam) pad |
| **Final Shot Aim & Shoot** | Drag & release from ball **OR** hold Space | Drag back & release |

## 🌟 Gameplay Features

1. **Rhythmic Dribble & Bounce Physics**:
   - The basketball bounces continuously with realistic elasticity and rotational spin.
   - **Squash & Stretch**: Dynamic cartoony deformation on court impact and launch.
   - **Ground Slam**: Slam downwards at top speed in midair to send shockwaves that crush nearby defenders.

2. **Obstacles & Hazards**:
   - **Spikes**: Floor and hanging ceiling hazards.
   - **Court Defenders**: Walkers, crawlers, and flying bats that patrol the courts. Stomp them from above for **+200 PTS**.
   - **Spring Trampolines**: Spring pads that launch the ball over tall walls and chasms.
   - **Moving Platforms**: Floating platforms bridging bottomless drops.

3. **🎯 The Climax: Buzzer Beater Final Shot**:
   - Every level culminates at the **Buzzer Beater Zone**.
   - Aim guide arc with power meter.
   - **Rim & Backboard Physics**: Realistic rebounds and rim rattles.
   - **Verlet Cloth Net Simulation**: Physical mesh net that reacts and ripples when the ball swishes through.
   - **Clean Swish Bonus**: +1000 PTS for a nothing-but-net shot!
   - Arena buzzer, referee whistle, crowd cheer, and confetti fireworks!

4. **3 Challenging Stages**:
   - **Level 1: Street Court Warmup**
   - **Level 2: Rooftop Jam**
   - **Level 3: Championship Arena**

5. **6 Selectable Ball Skins**:
   - Classic Basketball 🏀
   - Neon Volt Ball 🎾
   - Pro Soccer Ball ⚽
   - Lucky 8-Ball 🎱
   - Retro Beach Ball 🏐
   - Championship Baseball ⚾

6. **Web Audio API Synthesizer**:
   - Real court bounce thud, jump whoosh, rim ding, swish rustle, arena buzzer, and retro sports chiptune background groove.
